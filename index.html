Engineering approach

Where I draw the boundary between model behavior and software behavior.

LLMs are useful components, but they are unreliable places to hide application state or authorization.

  • 01 Treat model output as untrusted input.
  • 02 Keep application state outside conversation context.
  • 03 Put external side effects behind deterministic integrations and verify the result.
  • 04 Treat human handoff as a normal system outcome.

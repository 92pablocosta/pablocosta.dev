Engineering approach

Practical AI.
Real-world systems.

LLMs are useful components, but they are unreliable places to hide application state, authorization, or side effects.

  1. 01

    Treat model output as untrusted input.

    Validate, constrain, and expect unexpected output.

  2. 02

    Keep state outside the model.

    Conversation context is not application state.

  3. 03

    Make side effects deterministic.

    External actions should be explicit, observable, and verified.

  4. 04

    Design human handoff as an outcome.

    Escalation is part of the architecture, not a failure mode.
